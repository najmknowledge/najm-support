Overview

Najm is an Islamic trivia game. Najm itself does not require an account and does not collect or store your name, email address, account details, or gameplay history.

Information Najm collects

Najm does not directly collect or store personal information from players.

We do not use analytics services to track player behaviour, and we do not maintain player profiles or store player answers on our servers.

Advertising

Najm displays third-party advertisements. The advertising provider may process certain information automatically in order to deliver, measure, secure, or personalise ads, depending on your device settings and the provider's practices.

This may include:

  • device and app information;
  • IP address;
  • advertising identifiers or similar device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from network information;
  • information about ad impressions, clicks, or interactions.

Najm does not itself store this advertising data.
