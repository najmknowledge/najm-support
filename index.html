Need help with NAJM? You can find common answers below or contact us directly.

Gameplay help

NAJM is a five-question Islamic knowledge game. Type an accepted answer, submit it, and your rocket travels a distance based on the answer's rarity tier.

My answer was not recognised

Try a common English transliteration or spelling variation. If you believe a valid answer is missing, contact us and include the question and the answer you entered.

Something is not working

Please restart the app and make sure you are using the latest version. If the issue continues, tell us your device model, iOS version and what happened.

Contact support

Email us and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
